INFINIVERB
==========

Stereo Space Reverberation Unit, an algorithmic stereo reverb by fatbird Studios.
Version {{VERSION}}. https://fatbird-studios.com

System:    Windows 10 or later, 64-bit.
Format:    VST3.
Installs:  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\INFINIVERB.vst3
           Rescan plug-ins in your DAW if INFINIVERB does not appear.
Manual:    click the display to open the preset browser, then View Manual.
Removing:  Settings > Apps > INFINIVERB > Uninstall. Your own presets are kept.
